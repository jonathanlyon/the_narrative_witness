# Problem and proposition

**Status:** Foundation proposition for founder review and cohort validation  
**Evidence base:** Audience hypotheses, landscape scan, applicable privacy/data-governance guidance, and later repository audit  
**Not yet:** Final positioning, brand copy, clinical claim, or product-market fit

## The problem worth solving

People do not usually lack memories, material, or language. They lack a trustworthy practice that helps disparate life material become authored literary work.

The material may already exist as journal entries, voice notes, photographs, letters, research, half-drafts, remembered scenes, family accounts, and questions that will not go away. Current tools tend to separate the work into parts:

- capture tools store it;
- transcription tools turn speech into text;
- note systems link it;
- memoir services prompt it and print it;
- AI assistants summarise or generate from it;
- writing software organises a manuscript;
- courses, editors, and groups provide intermittent human attention.

The missing experience is continuity across **gathering, discerning, writing, and being witnessed**, with the source and the writer’s authority intact.

### Core problem statement

> When a person feels that lived material may contain a work, they need a private and sustained way to gather it, notice what may be forming, and write into it. Existing alternatives often require the writer to impose structure too early, fragment the work across tools, or hand interpretive and linguistic authority to automation or another person.

### The consequential harms behind the inconvenience

This is more than a productivity problem.

- **Loss of continuity:** the writer repeatedly reconstructs context, decisions, and emotional orientation.
- **Premature form:** a questionnaire, chapter template, or generic outline decides what matters before the writer does.
- **Provenance collapse:** quotation, recollection, external fact, family account, and AI inference become indistinguishable.
- **Authorship drift:** fluent generated prose can replace the struggle through which a writer discovers meaning and language.
- **False certainty:** an AI interpretation can sound authoritative about memory, motives, identity, or other people.
- **Relational harm:** a memoir can concern people and communities who did not choose to enter a software system.
- **Abandonment:** a person with meaningful material may never cross the threshold into a sustainable writing practice.

## Evidence, interpretation, and hypothesis

### Evidence

- Guided memoir products publicly organise the journey around prompts, easy capture, family participation, and a finished book. This demonstrates a market for lowered initiation friction and tangible outcomes. [Storyworth](https://help.storyworth.com/en_US/getting-started/what-is-a-storyworth-memoir-subscription), [Remento](https://help.remento.co/en/articles/8365873-what-is-remento-and-how-does-it-work), [Storii](https://www.storii.com/faqs), [Meminto](https://meminto.com/)
- Local and Australian memoir education explicitly teaches decisions about memory, scene, voice, point of view, time, structure, ethics, permissions, and publication. This demonstrates that serious memoir work exceeds memory capture. [NZSA](https://authors.org.nz/writer-toolkit/), [Faber Writing Academy](https://faberwritingacademy.com.au/course/writing-memoir-online-2026/)
- General source-grounded AI can constrain responses to supplied sources, while mature writing and knowledge tools already provide organisation, linking, snapshots, and export. These capabilities are precedents, not unique differentiators. [NotebookLM](https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/17003757), [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview), [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)
- New Zealand’s Privacy Commissioner explicitly warns that generative AI can produce confident factual or logical errors and notes access/correction rights for personal information. [Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz/resources-and-learning/a-z-topics/ai/generative-artificial-intelligence/)
- Te Mana Raraunga describes Māori data through authority, whakapapa, collective obligations, consent, respect, and guardianship. This means an individual account owner cannot always be treated as the only relevant rights-holder. [Principles](https://www.temanararaunga.maori.nz/s/TMR-Maori-Data-Sovereignty-Principles-Oct-2018.pdf)

### Interpretation

The category is optimised at two poles: **reduce the work until a keepsake appears**, or **increase the writer’s productive power**. A meaningful gap may exist between them for people who want to do the work, but cannot yet hold its volume, complexity, and uncertainty alone.

### Primary hypothesis

A writer will repeatedly choose and pay for a product that helps them perceive supported patterns and stay in relationship with a memoir project, provided it never obscures where an interpretation came from or who authors the work.

## The intended writer

The initial product is for an adult who:

- senses that a memoir, essay sequence, testimony, family history, or hybrid work may exist;
- has some lived material or can begin gathering it;
- wants to participate in shaping language and meaning rather than outsource a finished book;
- needs help with orientation, relationship, and continuity more than one-click prose;
- accepts carefully bounded AI involvement, or can use a meaningful AI-minimal route;
- can participate safely in a facilitated early cohort.

This includes not-yet writers as well as experienced writers. Writing credentials are irrelevant; desire for authorial participation is essential.

### Not the primary initial user

- Someone whose only desired outcome is the fastest possible gift book with no wish to write or shape it. Existing guided memoir services are likely a better fit.
- Someone seeking crisis help, trauma treatment, diagnosis, or confidential clinical care.
- Someone wanting AI to ghostwrite a complete memoir in an imitated voice.
- A publisher seeking automated manuscript acquisition or mass content production.
- A family purchaser who expects to control another person’s stories.
- A child or young person; safeguarding and consent requirements require a separate product decision.

## Jobs to be done

### Principal job

> Help me stay in authorship while I discover what my material may be becoming.

### Supporting jobs

- **When material is scattered,** help me gather and relate it without destroying its original context.
- **When I cannot see the work,** offer a provisional reading that I can inspect, challenge, and change.
- **When several directions are possible,** help me hold them as Story Seeds rather than force an outline.
- **When I begin writing,** keep the relevant material and decisions near without taking over the sentences.
- **When I return,** remember where the work and our understanding had reached.
- **When outside facts matter,** research only at my invitation and keep citations separate from memory and inference.
- **When I choose a reader,** help me share deliberately rather than expose the project by default.
- **When I leave,** give me an intelligible, portable body of my work and its provenance.

## The proposition

### Product proposition

> **The Narrative Witness is a private literary studio for gathering the materials of a life, sensing what may be forming across them, and writing the work in your own language—with a continuous Witness that remembers, shows its sources, and treats every interpretation as yours to accept, correct, or refuse.**

### Short internal expression

> **Not a ghostwriter. A witness to the work becoming.**

This line is a product test, not necessarily final marketing copy.

### What the writer receives

1. **A place for the work:** Ideas, original Fragments, linked references, Story Seeds, drafts, and revisions remain related and exportable.
2. **A way to see:** writer-invoked Sensing produces a versioned, source-grounded, provisional reading—not a verdict or replacement manuscript.
3. **A way to write:** selected context enters a stable Writing Pad without flooding the page or generating prose by default.
4. **A sustained reader:** the Witness remembers the project, asks live questions, respects boundaries, and distinguishes observation from fact.
5. **A possible path onward:** later Circle and Press experiences may support chosen readers, editorial development, and publication, but are not required for Studio value.

## The product’s five verbs

| Verb | Writer’s action | Product responsibility | Success is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Onboard** | Establish project, expectations, preferences, consent, and boundaries | Make authorship, AI use, privacy, support limits, and exit rights unmistakable | A marketing tour or blanket consent screen |
| **Gather** | Add or link text, voice, image/document, web, or existing work | Preserve originals, provenance, access limits, and relationships | An undifferentiated upload bin |
| **Sense** | Select material and ask what may be forming | Return versioned, provisional observations with “This came from” references and correction | A diagnosis, definitive thesis, or automatic outline |
| **Create** | Choose context and author the writing | Protect continuity, revisions, focus, and writer control | A one-click memoir generator |
| **Witness** | Enter an ongoing conversation with the work | Remember relevant context, ask, reflect, research when invited, and respect silence/uncertainty | Therapy, coaching authority, critique on demand, or simulated human intimacy without limits |

## Why the proposition could be distinctive

No single feature is enough. Source citations, voice capture, chat, project memory, notes, and writing pads are available elsewhere. The differentiation lies in their governing relationship:

```text
original material remains intact
        ↓
writer selects what is in view
        ↓
Witness offers a provisional, inspectable reading
        ↓
writer corrects, refuses, or preserves possibilities
        ↓
writer authors the work with chosen context
        ↓
the system remembers decisions without rewriting history
```

Competitors can copy interface elements. The harder capability is maintaining epistemic and authorial discipline across the complete experience: knowing what was supplied, what was inferred, what was researched, what the writer corrected, what may be shared, and which version shaped a later decision.

## Principles that must be experienced, not merely stated

### Writer authority

- The writer chooses the source set, timing, context, and next action.
- Generated prose is never passed off as the writer’s voice.
- Refusing an interpretation is a first-class action, not negative feedback hidden from view.
- Original sources are not altered when derived meaning changes.

### Provisional meaning

- Use language such as “I’m noticing,” “one possible reading,” and “these may be connected.”
- Make uncertainty visible.
- Do not infer diagnosis, trauma narrative, motive, identity, forgiveness, closure, or likely reader reaction as fact.
- Ask when a contradiction could reflect memory, chronology, metaphor, privacy, or a factual error.

### Provenance

- Exact quotations remain exact and point to a precise source location where practical.
- Writer statement, document fact, external fact, and Witness inference are visibly different.
- Every Sensing Document records its source set and version.
- Later ideas can link back without copying or relocating originals.

### Privacy and relational accountability

- Private by default and least privilege by design.
- AI processing is disclosed at the moment it matters, not only in terms of service.
- Access, correction, export, deletion, retention, and provider use are understandable.
- Material about other people receives prompts and controls proportionate to relational risk.
- Māori data requirements are governed with Māori expertise and authority; a generic privacy model is insufficient.

### Literary seriousness with a low threshold

- A person may begin with one fragment and no declared thesis.
- The system does not patronise beginners or bury them in writing-software machinery.
- The product values ambiguity, form, voice, silence, and revision rather than measuring success in generated word count.

## Explicit non-goals

### Not therapy

The product may feel reflective or meaningful. It must not claim to heal, treat, diagnose, regulate trauma, replace professional care, or provide crisis support. Cohort operations require boundaries and referral pathways precisely because memoir work can be emotionally intense.

### Not a ghostwriter

The Witness can ask, reflect, research, suggest possibilities, and provide editing when the writer explicitly requests it. It must not make invisible authorship changes or sell “a book in your voice” as its core outcome.

### Not a generic second brain

The Studio need not manage a person’s whole knowledge life. It supports memoir-related source, meaning, decisions, and writing. General task management, calendars, web clipping breadth, and plugin ecosystems are outside the first promise.

### Not an automatic biography service

Gathered answers do not become chapters by default. A chronology may be one useful view, but it does not define literary form.

### Not a social network or critique marketplace

Circle, if pursued, should be invitation-based, bounded, facilitated, and built around witnessing agreements. Feeds, follower counts, engagement loops, public profiles, and open critique are outside the proposition.

### Not a publishing shortcut

Press may later support editorial and production pathways. The Studio does not promise that material is publishable, legally cleared, ethically ready, or commercially viable.

## Positioning boundaries

| If a writer says… | Honest response |
|---|---|
| “I just want my parent to answer questions and receive a book.” | Recommend or compare a guided legacy service; do not force-fit this product. |
| “Write the whole story in my style.” | Outside the core proposition; explain the authorial boundary. |
| “Tell me what my trauma means.” | The Witness can reflect only on supplied writing provisionally; it cannot diagnose or provide therapy. |
| “Prove which family memory is true.” | Preserve accounts separately; offer writer-invoked cited research where appropriate; do not adjudicate lived testimony automatically. |
| “Can my whānau material be stored or analysed here?” | Do not answer with a generic yes. Establish authority, governance, consent, storage, and use requirements first. |
| “Will this get me published?” | It can help the work develop and export cleanly; publication requires separate editorial and publishing decisions. |

## First-cohort promise

The twelve-person cohort should make a modest, testable promise:

> Over a bounded programme, you will gather a meaningful body of material, discover and test possible threads within it, begin or deepen authored writing, and leave with your material, decisions, and next directions intact.

Do not promise a completed manuscript or book. The cohort should be considered successful only if it creates progress across three dimensions:

- **Practice:** the writer returns and can re-enter without reconstructing the project.
- **Discernment:** the writer makes a decision or sees a supported possibility they could not previously hold.
- **Authorship:** the writer experiences the resulting writing and direction as theirs.

## Proposition risks and falsification

| Risk | Why it matters | Falsifying evidence | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| “Witness” is beautiful language for ordinary chat | Brand promise exceeds product difference | General chatbot plus uploaded files performs as well on trust, usefulness, and return | Narrow or change the proposition; do not rely on tone alone |
| Sensing feels invasive | Interpretation arrives before permission or exceeds the material | Writers regularly report being read over, categorised, or emotionally manipulated | Reduce inference, increase source selection and controllable depth, revisit whether the feature should exist |
| Provenance becomes interface burden | Trust machinery interrupts writing | Writers cannot understand or ignore source treatment and fail core tasks | Layer detail progressively while preserving auditability |
| Cohort value comes only from the founder | Product appears successful but cannot stand alone | Engagement falls sharply outside facilitated sessions | Price and describe it as a programme or improve independent continuity before software scale |
| Mixed audiences require incompatible products | A single MVP becomes vague or inaccessible | Needs repeatedly conflict across capture, tone, pacing, and outcomes | Choose a narrower beachhead after cohort evidence |
| Writers actually want completion delegated | Authorial proposition serves too small a segment | Most participants prefer generated chapters and book fulfilment | Do not imitate legacy-book leaders poorly; reconsider target market or remain a specialist literary service |
| Sensitive material makes AI unacceptable | Core Sense/Witness loop cannot earn consent | Material is withheld, AI-minimal path is consistently preferred, or governance cannot be met | Make human/manual pathways primary or stop serving affected contexts until requirements are satisfied |

## Decisions the founder can make now

- Use **writer-authored, source-traceable literary meaning-making** as the foundation hypothesis.
- Treat the complete system—not AI generation—as the prospective differentiation.
- Optimise the first cohort for learning and safe progress, not manuscript completion.
- Keep Circle and Press as future pathways, not requirements for proving Studio.
- Be willing to refer a customer to Storyworth, Remento, a writing course, an editor, or professional support when those better match the job.

## Decisions to defer until evidence exists

- Final primary audience and buyer.
- Consumer subscription price.
- Whether cohort and software are one offer or separate products.
- Whether voice recording requires native capture in the first safe vertical slice.
- Whether a printed object belongs in the core offer.
- Whether Circle should be cohort continuity, curated readers, peer witnessing, or none of these.
- Whether Press is a service, partner network, imprint, or simply an export/readiness standard.

## Decision test

Every proposed feature, message, and commercial choice should answer:

1. Does this help the writer remain in relationship with the work?
2. Does it preserve or increase writer authority?
3. Can the writer see what came from where?
4. Is an inference presented with proportionate uncertainty?
5. Does this belong in software, facilitation, expert service, or an external partner?
6. If removed, would the differentiated promise materially weaken?

If a feature mainly increases content volume, engagement, or apparent intelligence while failing those tests, it is not part of the foundation proposition.
